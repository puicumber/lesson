Mystery gifts

Guessing game

But first: ID check!

The security guard at the party door doesn't believe this ID. Look carefully and help him:

  • Is this ID real? "It can't be real, because…"
  • How old must the person on the card be, according to the date of birth?
  • Find at least five differences between the two cards.
  • What might the person's real name be?

Use must / might / can't in every answer.

Six presents for Diana are shaking under the table! Read the clues and guess what's inside: "It must be…", "It might be…", "It can't be…". Then open the box.

What if…?

First and second conditional
If + present, willReal and possible: If it snows tomorrow, I'll stay at home.
If + past, wouldImaginary: If I met my favourite idol, I'd probably cry.

Pop the balloons

Take turns popping a balloon and answering the question. Pink balloons are imaginary (would), green balloons are real (will).
