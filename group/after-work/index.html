What do the phrases mean?

Find each phrase in the messages. Guess the meaning, then open the card. Choose three you could use this week.

pick up some groceries

Buy some food to take home. Купить продукты по пути.

make something quick

Make food that takes a short time. Приготовить что-нибудь на скорую руку.

have an early night

Go to bed early. Лечь спать пораньше.

get changed

Put on different clothes. Переодеться.

order takeaway

Order food from a restaurant to eat at home. Заказать готовую еду.

feel like watching a comedy

Want to watch a comedy. Хочется посмотреть комедию.
