Side A · Track 4

Make a Mixtape

Each of you makes a tape for one character. Choose three to five real songs (any language, any decade) and explain each choice in one sentence. Then present your tape: "I chose this song because Sam has always… If Charlie heard it, he would…"

Side A · Track 5

Infinite

In the book, Charlie talks about feeling "infinite". What do you think he means?

Take your time. Tell a real story: when, where, who was there, what happened.

Side B · Bonus track

Invent a Sport

Roll the machine, then invent a new sport together. Explain the rules: what you must / mustn't / don't have to do, and where the ball and players go (across, over, through, into, round…). Give your sport a name!
