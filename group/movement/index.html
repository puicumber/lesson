Out and About

Take Mia and her dog Biscuit for a walk around town, and they'll go exactly where you send them. Then practise the tricky pairs, sports and a football commentary.

Step 1

The walk

Choose the right word, and watch Mia and Biscuit move. Wrong word? They'll stay where they are.

    Step 2

    Tricky pairs

    into / out of + nounCome into the kitchen. He went out of the house.No noun? in / out: Come in! He went out.
    across / throughacross: from one side to the other of something flat (a road, a river, a square).through: inside something (a tunnel, a forest, a crowd).
    come / gocome: towards the speaker. go: away from the speaker.Come here! Go away!
    away / backaway: to another place. back: to the place where you were before.The dog ran away and never came back.
    Step 3

    Play, go or do?

    playsports with a ball: play tennis, play football
    gosports ending in -ing: go cycling, go skiing
    doother sports and exercise: do yoga, do karate
