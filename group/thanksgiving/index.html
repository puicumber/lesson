The story behind Thanksgiving

Watch What Is Thanksgiving? on YouTube

First watch. Which three topics does the video talk about? Dates · history · food · music · sports

Second watch. Both of you answer the same questions. Write a few words, then compare answers.

  1. When is Thanksgiving in the US?
  2. Where did the 1621 harvest celebration take place?
  3. Who took part? How long did it last?
  4. What’s the main dish? Can you name two other foods from the video?
Check the answers
  1. The fourth Thursday in November.
  2. Plymouth.
  3. English settlers and Wampanoag people took part. The celebration lasted three days.
  4. Turkey. Other foods mentioned include stuffing, mashed potatoes, gravy, cranberry sauce and pumpkin pie.
The Wampanoag already had their own traditions of giving thanks. The 1621 harvest celebration is one part of the story of Thanksgiving. Plimoth Patuxet Museums

    After dinner

    One of you is the host and one is the guest. Use a dish from your menu, then swap roles and choose a different dish.

    Guest

    Thank the host, name your favourite dish and ask a question about it. Accept or politely decline the offer to take some home.

    Host

    Answer your guest’s question, then offer some leftovers to take home.
