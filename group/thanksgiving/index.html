The story behind Thanksgiving

Watch What Is Thanksgiving? on YouTube

First watch. Which three topics does the video talk about? Dates · history · food · music · sports

Second watch. Both of you answer the same questions. Write a few words, then compare answers.

  1. When is Thanksgiving in the US?
  2. Where did the 1621 harvest celebration take place?
  3. Who took part? How long did it last?
  4. What’s the main dish? Can you name two other foods from the video?
Check the answers
  1. The fourth Thursday in November.
  2. Plymouth.
  3. English settlers and Wampanoag people took part. The celebration lasted three days.
  4. Turkey. Other foods mentioned include stuffing, mashed potatoes, gravy, cranberry sauce and pumpkin pie.
The Wampanoag already had their own traditions of giving thanks. The 1621 harvest celebration is one part of the story of Thanksgiving. Plimoth Patuxet Museums

Plan the menu

Match each dish to its photo and description. Then choose what to serve with the turkey.

    Guest A

    Choose something you’ve never tried. Ask your partner about it before you decide.

    What does it taste like? What would you serve it with?

    Guest B

    Suggest something that reminds you of family celebrations. Explain why you’d serve it.

    We usually have … at family celebrations. Could we add it to our menu?

    Your dinner menu

    Agree on two different sides, a sauce and a dessert. You already have roast turkey.

    Present your menu together. Each of you explains one choice. We chose … because …

    Optional · A guest is coming

    Guest A, you’ve invited a vegetarian friend. Ask which dishes they can eat. Guest B, suggest a change to your menu. Then agree on one dish to offer.

    Is the gravy made with meat? · Could we serve … instead? · Would you like some …?

    Say thanks

    I’m thankful for my friend because she always makes me laugh.

    Pick a prompt, take a moment to think, then say your thanks aloud.

    Let’s eat!

    Take turns. Pick a piece, answer the question and let your partner ask one follow-up.

    Guest A, choose the first piece.

    Follow-up ideas · Who usually makes it? Have you ever tried it? What would you do next?

    After dinner

    One of you is the host and one is the guest. Use a dish from your menu, then swap roles and choose a different dish.

    Guest

    Thank the host, name your favourite dish and ask a question about it. Accept or politely decline the offer to take some home.

    Host

    Answer your guest’s question, then offer some leftovers to take home.
