Type 1 · no object

Never split.

I get up at 7. Please sit down.

Type 2 · + object, can split

A noun can go in the middle or after. A pronoun (it, them, her…) must go in the middle.

Turn off the TV. Turn the TV off. Turn it off. Turn off it.

Type 3 · + object, never split

The object always goes after the whole verb.

Look for your keys. Look for them. Look them for.
