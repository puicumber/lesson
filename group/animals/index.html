Animal Field Guide

Learn 33 animals, then practise the present perfect with for and since. Everything checks itself.

Step 1

Flashcards

Look at the picture and say the animal out loud. Click to check. "Got it" removes the card; "Again" keeps it.

Step 2

Sort the animals

Where does each animal belong: insects, farm animals, wild animals or sea animals?

Step 3

Spell it

Type the animal. Careful with the tricky ones! Press Enter or "Check all".

    Step 4

    Bite, sting and plurals

    stingBees, wasps and jellyfish sting: they inject venom into your skin.
    biteMosquitoes, spiders, snakes and all animals with teeth bite.
    irregular pluralsone mouse, two mice · one deer, two deer · one sheep, two sheep
    Step 5

    for or since?

    for+ a period of timefor two weeks, for ten years, for ages, for a long time
    since+ the start of the periodsince 2015, since last June, since Monday, since I was a child
    Step 6

    Present perfect: how long?

    It started in the past and is still true now
    2015now
    I've had my cat since 2015. = I got her in 2015 and I still have her.
    Not the present simple!✗ I have my cat for ten years.
    ✓ I've had my cat for ten years.    Question: How long have you had your cat?
