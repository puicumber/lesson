How long has this been going on?

Present perfect vs present perfect continuous
have doneThe result, how many, how much: I've broken my wrist. I've taken three pills today.
Stative verbs: I've had this rash since Halloween.
have been doingThe process and how long: I've been coughing for a week.
Visible evidence: Your eyes are red. Have you been crying?

Prepositions of time and place: the tricky ones
at nightduring the night: Ghosts appear at night. I can't sleep at night. (But: in the morning, in the evening.)
on time / in timeon time: at the planned time. in time: early enough, not too late.
at the end / in the endat the end of something: the last part. in the end: finally, after everything.
