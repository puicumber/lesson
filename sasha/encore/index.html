Read about Sixto Rodriguez

Sixto Rodriguez began performing in the 1960s and recorded two albums, Cold Fact and Coming From Reality, early in the next decade. By the mid-1970s, he had largely left professional music and was earning a living as a labourer in Detroit.

While he was working in Detroit, his records were finding an audience overseas. In South Africa, his songs became associated with opposition to apartheid. Some listeners had heard false rumours that he had died.

In 1997, two South African fans set out to find him. They eventually learned where he was through one of his daughters. Rodriguez later toured South Africa, where he performed for thousands of fans who had been listening to his music for years.

Rodriguez recorded two albums early in his career. By the mid-1970s, he’d mostly given up professional music and was working as a labourer in Detroit. Meanwhile, his songs were growing popular in South Africa, where some fans had heard a rumour that he’d died. In 1997, two fans decided to track him down and eventually made contact through one of his daughters. When he toured South Africa, he finally met people who’d been listening to his records for years.
