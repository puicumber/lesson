PAPER 1 Reading & Use of English

CPE Use of English

Four exam parts, original C2 items on psychology and mental health. Do each part, check, read the explanations, and try again until you score 80% or more.

Part 1 · Multiple-choice cloze

Burnout on the ward

Read the text, then choose A, B, C or D for each gap. Only your first try counts towards the score.

Part 2 · Open cloze

The first session

Write one word in each gap. There's no word bank, just like in the exam.

    Part 3 · Word formation

    Change the word

    Use the word in capitals to form a word that fits the gap.

    4 steps: part of speech → suffix → positive or negative (prefix)? → spelling and plural. Words with -miss- and -cess- take -ible and the prefix in-.

      Part 4 · Key word transformations

      Say it another way

      Complete the second sentence so that it means the same as the first. Use 3–8 words, including the word in capitals, and don't change that word. A contraction (didn't) counts as two words.

      Read the second sentence to the end before you write. Your gap has to join up with the words after it.
