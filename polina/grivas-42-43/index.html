The Late Edition

You’re on the copy desk. Get tomorrow’s edition ready without changing what the sources meant.

Quick review

Emphasis and attitude

By no means means certainly not. “The verdict was by no means unanimous.”

Take notice of means pay attention to. “The editors took notice of the readers’ complaints.”

Take someone or something for granted means fail to appreciate them or it. “Don’t take your volunteers for granted.”

Have had enough of means no longer be willing to tolerate something. “We’ve had enough of last-minute changes.”

Preferences, decisions and suitability

Have a preference for + noun or -ing. “I have a preference for publishing interviews in full.”

Be in the mood to + infinitive. “I’m in the mood to work on something lighter.”

Have no option but to + infinitive expresses necessity. “We had no option but to withdraw the article.”

There is no question of + -ing rules out an action. “There’s no question of naming the witness.”

Hand in your resignation means formally resign. “The editor handed in her resignation on Monday.”

Be cut out for + noun or -ing / be cut out to be + role means have the right qualities. “She’s cut out for investigative work. She’s cut out to be a correspondent.”

Reactions, evidence and comparisons

Meet with approval means get a favourable response. “The revised headline met with the editor’s approval.”

Show no sign of + noun or -ing means give no indication. “The rain showed no sign of letting up.”

Bear a resemblance to means be similar to. “The new logo bears a resemblance to the old masthead.”

Be taken by surprise means be caught off guard. “The reporters were taken by surprise at the early announcement.”

Find + object + difficult + to-infinitive. “I find the recordings difficult to date.”

Little value leaves room for some usefulness. “The undated clipping is of little value, though it confirms the venue.” No value rules out any usefulness for the stated purpose. “The blank sheet is of no value as evidence.”

Advanced Language Points

Usage and collocation notes

Use pay the driver for the recipient, pay the bill for the amount owed, and pay for the ride for the thing you buy. Pay for a mistake means suffer its consequences.

The very person means exactly the person. You’re being jealous comments on present behaviour. Went to sleep at ten gives the time sleep began.

Consistent with means compatible with the evidence. It leaves room for other explanations. Have a feel for means have an intuitive understanding or knack. Feel for someone means sympathise with them.

Bonus · Eleven hads

Punctuation changes what Mary and Paul appear to have written. Add punctuation without removing or moving any words.

One way to punctuate it

Mary, where Paul had had “had had”, had had “had”. “Had had” had had the teacher’s approval.
  • Mary wrote “had”.
  • Paul wrote “had had”.
  • The teacher approved Paul’s “had had”.

Inside the quotation marks, had and had had are the answers the students wrote. Outside, had had tells us what each person had written or which answer had received approval.
