Been There, Done That

Two tenses, one simple question: do we say when? Learn the rule, then practise it seven different ways until it feels automatic.

Step 1

The rule

Read this first. Everything else in this homework comes back to it.

present perfecthave / has + past participleI've seen · She's bought · They haven't finished We don't say when. What matters is the experience or the result now. I've been to Italy twice. (in my life) · I've lost my keys! (I can't open the door now)
past simpleworked, went, saw, bought…I saw · She bought · They didn't finish We say or know when. It's finished, in the past. I went to Italy in 2019. · I lost my keys last week.

MashaHave you ever been to a concert abroad?PP: experience, no time

VladaYes, I have. I went to Riga last summer.PS: now we say when

MashaWho did you see?PS: we're talking about that concert

Typical pattern: start with the present perfect (Have you ever…?), then continue with the past simple for the details (When? Where? Who?).
