Room 4 · The Hallway

Where is the ghost?

Prepositions of place and time: in / on / at
inPlace: inside something. in the attic, in a box, in Moscow
Time: months, years, seasons, parts of the day. in October, in 1887, in the morning
onPlace: on a surface. on the wall, on the roof, on the sofa
Time: days and dates. on Friday, on October 31st, on Saturday evening
atPlace: a point or an event. at the door, at the bus stop, at a party
Time: clock times and short moments. at 9 p.m., at midnight, at night

      Room 5 · The Fortune Teller's Room

      What does the future hold?

      Future forms: will / be going to / present continuous
      willPredictions and opinions: You will meet a stranger. I think it'll rain.
      Decisions now: I'm cold. I'll close the window.
      be going toPlans and intentions: I'm going to learn to drive.
      Evidence now: Look at the clouds! It's going to rain.
      present continuousFixed arrangements with a time or place: I'm meeting Anna at 7. We're flying to Paris tomorrow.
