Step 0

The map

Read this first. Come back here any time you're not sure.

Present simpleI work · she works · do you work? Habits, routines, facts.I go to the gym twice a week. Water boils at 100°. 🔑 usually, often, every day, on Mondays, never
Present continuousI'm working · she's working Now, at the moment, or a temporary situation.Shh! I'm talking on the phone. She's staying with her aunt this month. 🔑 now, right now, at the moment, Look! Listen!
Past simpleI worked · I went · did you go? Finished actions. We say or know when.I saw Anna yesterday. We went to Turkey in 2023. 🔑 yesterday, last week, two days ago, in 2019, When…?
Present perfectI've seen · she's been · have you ever…? Experiences (no time), a result now, or non-action verbs from the past until now.I've never been to Japan. I've lost my keys! We've known each other for years. 🔑 ever, never, just, already, yet, for, since
Present perfect continuousI've been working · has she been waiting? An action that started in the past and is still going on (how long?), or a recent action with a result we can see.I've been learning English since September. Your eyes are red! Have you been crying? 🔑 How long…? for, since, all day, recently
The future

be going to = a plan you decided before, or what we can see will happen: I'm going to learn to drive. Look at those clouds, it's going to rain!

present continuous = an arrangement (time, place, other people): I'm meeting Anna at 7 tonight.

will = a decision now, a promise, an offer, or an opinion about the future: I'll get it! I'll help you. I think it'll be sunny.

⚠️ Non-action verbs (know, like, love, want, need, understand, believe, have = own) don't usually take -ing: I'm knowing → I know · I've been knowing → I've known.
