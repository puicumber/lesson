Room 3 · The Museum of Dental Horrors

It was believed to be caused by worms

Passive voice

Dr Fang keeps a small museum in the basement. Read the three exhibits.

Exhibit 1

The tooth worm

For thousands of years, toothache was believed to be caused by tiny worms living inside the tooth. The idea was first recorded in ancient Babylon, and it was still taken seriously by some doctors in the 18th century.

Exhibit 2

Barber-surgeons

In medieval Europe, teeth were usually pulled by barbers, who also cut hair and let blood. No anaesthetic was used. The red and white barber's pole is said to represent blood and bandages.

Exhibit 3

Waterloo teeth

After the Battle of Waterloo in 1815, thousands of teeth were taken from dead soldiers and sold to dentists. They were made into dentures, which became known as "Waterloo teeth". Teeth were also stolen from graves by body snatchers.

Adjectives vs adverbs
adjectiveDescribes a noun, and comes after linking verbs: look, seem, feel, sound, smell, taste, become. The room smelled strange.
adverbDescribes how something is done: The dentist smiled strangely.
