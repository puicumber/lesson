Your turn: out loud

Talk me through it

A nervous patient needs a wisdom tooth out. Explain the procedure out loud, one step at a time. Use be going to, a What… sentence and at least two softer words. No writing: just say it, and we'll do it together in the next lesson.

  1. Say what the procedure is and why the patient needs it. It's a very straightforward procedure. What we're going to do is…
  2. Explain the anaesthetic. All you'll feel is a little pinprick…
  3. Explain how the tooth comes out. What happens next is that… You might feel a little bit of pressure, but…
  4. Explain the stitches. After that, we're going to… You'll barely notice them.
  5. Say what happens afterwards and how long it takes. It just takes… You'll feel a little sore for a day or two.
